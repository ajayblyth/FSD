OJT Daily Log
A simple daily log. Each member records what they did each day, mentors leave comments.

Set up your team once
. Each team has its own tab (Team 1, Team 2, ... ). Rename the tab to your real team name.
. At the top of your team tab, type your members' names in the Members box. These names then appear in
the Member dropdown in the log.

Log your work each day
. Add one row for what you did. Fill in the Date, pick your Name, pick the Project, write what you did, set a
Status, and add any Notes.
. Add a fresh row each day for each thing you worked on. Everyone on the team logs into the same team tab.
. The Status is a dropdown (Not Started, In Progress, Done, Blocked) and the row colours itself.

For mentors
. The last column, Mentor Comments, is for the mentor's feedback on an entry. Members leave this column
blank

Columns
. Date: the day you did the work.
. Member: pick your name from the dropdown.
. Project: which of the three projects this belongs to.
. What I did: a short description of the task or progress.
. Status: Not Started, In Progress, Done, or Blocked.
. Notes: anything useful, like a blocker, a link, or a question.
. Mentor Comments: filled in by the mentor.
